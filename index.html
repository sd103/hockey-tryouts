Welcome! Here's how it works

  • Pick an age group (M11, M13...) using the buttons at the top.
  • Pick the session — each age group opens on Avg (your own average across sessions, read-only). Tap S1 / S2 / S3 to score the session you're watching; it stays selected for that age group until you reload (other age groups can be on a different session).
  • Score players right in the table — tap the dropdowns to give 1–5 for Overall, Skating, Compete and Hockey IQ. 1 = best, 5 = worst. Scores save the moment you pick them. Leave anything you didn't see blank — blanks are never averaged in.
  • Jamboree (M11 only) has its own spot on the switch. It's one score per player, kept completely separate from the sessions.
  • Add notes with the pencil — tap the ✎ next to a player's name, type your observation and tap Add note. A timestamp is added automatically, and you can keep adding entries as the tryout goes on. The pencil turns dark when notes exist. To fix a mistake in a note, tap the player's name and edit the notes on their scoring page.
  • Goalies have their own table below the skaters in each age group — don't forget them! Their skills are scored as Skill 1, 2, 3.
  • Tap a player's name to open the full scoring page. It opens on your Average; tap S1, S2 or S3 there to score every skill for that player in one place.
  • Use the filters — position, an Overall range, and No Overall Score, which lists the players you haven't given an Overall yet in the selected session. Check it before you leave the rink.
  • You can change any score at any time — just tap the dropdown again, or use the – option to clear it.
  • Bad rink wifi? No problem. Scores save on your phone and send automatically when connection returns. A banner shows anything still waiting.
  • Only you and the admin see your scores. Other evaluators can't.
